# LAY/ERD

A perspective on money, decisions, and the tech behind both.

LAY/ERD isn't trying to turn anyone into an expert. It's here to close the gap
between the money decisions people make every day and the stuff nobody ever
actually sat them down and explained — personal finance as a baseline, not a
nice-to-have, whether you're an analyst, a student, or a founder.

Money and security go hand in hand. Every account you open, every app you
hand your balance to, every password you reuse is a security decision as much
as a financial one. So the site covers both sides: the personal finance
fundamentals, and the security fundamentals that decide whether any of it's
actually safe. Fundamentals first, then more technical deep-dives once that
foundation is there.

Written by Tayo — B.B.A in Cybersecurity student, former AML Analyst — at the
overlap of how money moves, what actually protects it, and the mechanics
behind fraud.
